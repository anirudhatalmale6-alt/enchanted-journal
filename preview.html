The 3D journal

A real three-dimensional book: the boards, the spine and every leaf you turn are solid, and the light is really in the scene, so the shadow under the page moves as the page does. Drag to see it from another angle.

There are two settings, switched in the top right corner — candlelight on a dark walnut table, and a daylight sky with the book resting on a sea of cloud. Same book, same words; pick whichever you prefer.

Every daily inspiration is on the right-hand page, centred, with an inch of margin all round. The numbered tabs down the side jump straight to a day.
